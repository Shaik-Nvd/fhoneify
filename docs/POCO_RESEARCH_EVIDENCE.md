## Calibration proposal and validation gate

Do not change `lib/pricingCalculator.ts`, uplift rules, or model-specific constants on this evidence. First obtain paired Cashify/Fhoneify questionnaire answer logs or sequential screenshots for the same device/variant, time, and location. Record the Cashify Get Upto headline separately from the final offer. Normalize the two sites' answer semantics and compare Fhoneify's *pre-uplift* Cashify-equivalent to Cashify's final offer; report customer-quote gaps separately. Keep whole devices in either fit or untouched validation, never both.

For each selected POCO family and variant, collect a verified clean baseline, then one-at-a-time changes with all other answers held fixed: warranty (only when actually asked), age/bill control (only when asked), original versus local display, cracked screen, screen spots/lines, body scratches/dents/panel, and each functional defect. Repeat at multiple price bands before deciding flat-rupee versus percentage deduction or double age depreciation. To distinguish additive from multiplicative deductions, collect baseline, A-only, B-only, and A+B on the **same** device for screen+body and screen+functional pairs; add original/local display × screen damage and warranty × age where the questions exist. Three profiles per device (clean, one control, one screen defect) cannot identify these interaction terms or generalize to unobserved conditions. A future proposal must fit only on the training-device set and report untouched validation MAE, median absolute error, percentage error, under/over counts, and family/condition breakdown before any pricing change is considered.
