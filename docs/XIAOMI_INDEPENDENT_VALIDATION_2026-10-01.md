## Redmi Note 15 Pro+ production gate

At 12 GB/512 GB and same-day Get Upto INR 29,250, the clean Selling price was INR 21,780. Cracked glass with working touch and warranty No sold for INR 19,100, a INR 2,680 deduction and 65.3% of Get Upto. The old percentage engine predicts INR 14,449; the corrected INR engine predicts INR 19,720, or 67.4% of Get Upto. The original frozen same-day control formula predicts INR 19,470 (INR 370 over, 1.94%). Touch failure alone sold for INR 17,280; the old engine predicts INR 9,038 and corrected INR INR 18,060 (INR 780 over, 4.51%). The Note opening and closing clean Selling controls both returned INR 21,780 with Get Upto INR 29,250.

The reported ~93% retention under cracked glass is the **nominally in-warranty** INR case. This job captured cracked glass **out of warranty**, so its 65.3% retention does not verify that in-warranty claim. An eight-fault profile was preregistered to exercise the cap (old prediction INR 380; pre-fix INR INR 14,450; corrected INR INR 14,560 at the original INR 28,100 reference), but Cashify showed no final Selling price. Neither coefficient fitting nor an inferred zero quote is justified. Keep this model blocked from production activation until a matched in-warranty clean/cracked pair and a Cashify final price for a supported severe-fault profile can be obtained automatically in a separately authorized attempt budget.
