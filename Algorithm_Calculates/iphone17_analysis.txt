# iPhone 17 Cashify Pricing Analysis

## Storage Variant "Get Upto" Prices
- 256 GB: ₹56,500
- 512 GB: ₹65,000

## Baseline Configuration (Flawless)
- Calls: Yes
- Touch: Yes
- Original Screen: Yes
- Warranty: Yes
- GST Bill: Yes
- eSIM: Single

## Observations
- The jump from 256GB to 512GB is ₹8,500 (~15% increase), which is more significant than the iPhone 14 Pro Max's storage delta.
- Cashify now explicitly asks for GST bill and Warranty for the iPhone 17, reinforcing the "New Model" logic identified in the iPhone 16 analysis.
- The base price for a 256GB iPhone 17 is higher than the 1TB iPhone 14 Pro Max, showing the premium for the latest generation.
