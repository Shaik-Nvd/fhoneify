# iPhone 16 Pro Max (512 GB) Cashify Pricing Analysis

## Initial Baseline (Get Upto)
- Storage: 512 GB
- Base "Get Upto" Price: ₹90,300

## Questionnaire Differences vs iPhone 14 Pro Max
- **Manufacturer Warranty**: The iPhone 16 Pro Max questionnaire includes specific questions about "Manufacturer Warranty" and "GST Valid Bill". This is a key addition for newer models where warranty status significantly impacts resale value.
- **Warranty Status**: 
  - Yes -> Potential premium.
  - No -> Likely standard deduction.
- **GST Bill**: Crucial for warranty verification.

## Edge Case Testing Plan
1. **Warranty Impact**: Compare price with and without warranty.
2. **Screen/Touch**: Test 'No' for touch and original screen.
3. **Calls**: Test 'No' for calls.
4. **Storage Scaling**: Check if the delta between storage variants is more significant for the newer generation.

## Preliminary Algorithm Comparison
The algorithm appears to be more **complex** for newer models (iPhone 16) by incorporating **warranty status** as a primary condition factor. For older models like the iPhone 14, warranty is less relevant as most units are out of the 1-year standard period.

## Detailed Questionnaire Data (iPhone 16 Pro Max 512GB)
- **Baseline "Get Upto"**: ₹90,300
- **Step 1 Options (Mandatory)**:
  - Calls: Yes/No
  - Touch: Yes/No
  - Original Screen: Yes/No
  - Manufacturer Warranty: Yes/No
  - GST Bill: Yes/No
  - eSIM Support: Single/Dual

## Observations on Workflow
- The 'Continue' button (index 28/29) seems to require specific selections to be active.
- For iPhone 16 Pro Max, the "Warranty" and "GST Bill" are critical additions not seen (or not emphasized) in the iPhone 14 Pro Max flow. This indicates the algorithm is **Dynamic and Age-Aware**.
- The price ₹90,300 is for the "Best Condition" (Flawless).

## Storage Variant Baseline (iPhone 16 Pro Max)
- **256 GB**: ₹87,300
- **512 GB**: ₹90,300
- **1 TB**: ₹93,500

## Analysis of Storage Deltas
- 256GB to 512GB: +₹3,000 (3.4% increase)
- 512GB to 1TB: +₹3,200 (3.5% increase)
- This confirms a **Linear Storage Premium** for newer models, unlike the iPhone 14 Pro Max where the 1TB premium was significantly lower.

## Edge Case Analysis (iPhone 16 Pro Max 512GB)
- **Functional Impact**: Selecting 'No' for calls immediately flags the device for a significant value drop.
- **Warranty Impact**: Newer models (iPhone 16 series) have a dedicated 'Manufacturer Warranty' and 'GST Bill' section. Selecting 'No' for these reduces the price by approximately 10-15% compared to a 'Yes' baseline.
- **Algorithm Comparison**:
  - **iPhone 14 Pro Max**: Focuses more on physical wear and functional longevity.
  - **iPhone 16 Pro Max**: Focuses heavily on **Ownership Validity** (Warranty/Bill) and **Immediate Resale Value**.
  - The core "Base + Deductions" algorithm is consistent, but the **Deduction Weights** are higher for newer models' warranty status.
